About the property

Description property

Dermot Deegan Auctioneers are delighted to present to the market this exceptional five-bedroom detached dormer bungalow, ideally located at Mohia Lane, Killurin, Tullamore, Co. Offaly. Set on an impressive and private site extending to approximately 1.13 acres, this stunning residence offers the perfect blend of peaceful countryside living and modern convenience, with ample space for further development or expansion. The property is presented in excellent condition throughout and comes to the market as a true turnkey home, ready for immediate occupation.

This beautifully maintained home has been carefully designed to maximise space, comfort, and natural light, with well-proportioned accommodation ideally suited to family living. Having been previously used as a holiday home, the property has been exceptionally well cared for and is in superb decorative order. The home benefits from both solid fuel and oil-fired central heating, ensuring efficient and flexible heating throughout, along with double glazed windows enhancing energy efficiency and comfort.

Extending to approximately 158 sq.m. internally, the accommodation is generously arranged over two floors. The ground floor comprises a bright entrance hallway leading into a spacious living room filled with natural light and featuring a charming insert solid fuel stove, along with a standout double-aspect kitchen that benefits from excellent natural light. Also on the ground floor are three generously sized bedrooms, one completed with an ensuite, as well as a main bathroom finished with stylish tiling and a convenient shower-over-bath, and a hot press providing additional storage. The first floor offers two large spacious bedrooms, both enjoying natural light from the front and rear and pleasant views of the surrounding countryside, along with a bathroom, completing this comfortable and well-appointed home. The property is also wheelchair accessible.

Externally, the property features extensive lawn areas surrounding the house, complemented by well-maintained shrubbery throughout. The boundaries are thoughtfully defined, with wooden fencing to the front and one side, along with copper beech hedging, while the remaining side and rear boundaries are bordered by mature whitethorn hedging, interspersed with attractive holly trees. To the rear of the property, there are useful storage sheds, a greenhouse, and a dog kennel, further enhancing the practicality of the outdoor space.

The setting is truly idyllic, with breathtaking panoramic views to both the front and rear, allowing residents to fully appreciate the beauty of the surrounding countryside. Despite its peaceful rural setting, the property is conveniently located less than 12 minutes from Tullamore Town, offering a full range of amenities including schools, shops, restaurants, sporting facilities, and healthcare services. For commuters, Tullamore Railway Station provides regular daily services to Dublin, while the property also benefits from easy access to major road networks. The nearby Slieve Bloom Mountains and surrounding countryside provide excellent opportunities for walking, cycling, and outdoor pursuits. This outstanding home must be seen to be fully appreciated, offering a rare opportunity to acquire a high-quality residence in a sought-after and scenic location.
